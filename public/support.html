Support

Greenlit scans food and tells you whether it is safe for you, with a personal health score.

Need help or found a problem?

Report it here and we will reply: open a support request.

A product says “not in the database”

Barcode data comes from Open Food Facts, a volunteer-run database. Coverage is best in Europe and North America. For anything missing, use “Photograph the label” — it reads the ingredients in any language.

How do I turn on label photos and AI explanations?

They are free extras that use Google’s AI with your own free key. In the app go to Settings → Set up AI extras and follow the four steps. It takes about a minute and needs no card.

The camera will not open

Allow camera access for Greenlit in your phone’s settings. You can always type the barcode digits instead.

The result looks wrong

Product data can be incomplete or out of date, and recipes change. Always read the physical label, especially if you have an allergy or a medical condition. Greenlit is not medical advice.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Delete all my data. Everything Greenlit stores is on your device; nothing is kept on a server.

Is it really free?

Yes. No subscriptions, no ads, no paid features.
